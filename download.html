Why Does Windows Warn You?

When you run Network Unity Chat for the first time, Windows may show a blue screen saying "Windows Protected Your PC." This is normal and does not mean the app is a virus. Here is why it happens and how to get past it.

🛡

Why SmartScreen Appears

Windows Defender SmartScreen — what it is and why it fires

Windows SmartScreen flags any app that does not have a code-signing certificate from Microsoft. These certificates cost hundreds of dollars per year and are only worth it for large commercial software. Network Unity Chat is a free indie app, so it is not signed — which is completely normal for free tools.

SmartScreen does not scan the file for viruses. It just checks whether the developer paid for a certificate. The warning does not mean the app is dangerous. If you are still unsure, scroll down — you can try the chat in your browser first before installing anything.

✅

How to Run It Anyway
